Figma Sub-processors
Last Updated:
August 7, 2026
This page lists the sub-processors that Figma engages to process Customer Content (as defined in
Figma’s Data Processing Addendum
) on behalf of Customers and to support the delivery of our offerings, products and services (“Services”).
The sub-processors relevant to a particular Customer depend on the specific Services they use.
Figma requires each sub-processor to implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure that Customer Content is protected as required under applicable data protection laws.
When the use of a sub-processor involves the cross-border transfer of Customer Content, Figma conducts transfer impact assessments and ensures an appropriate transfer mechanism is in place.
Figma maintains an up-to-date list of all sub-processors below, including a description of their processing activities, the categories of personal data they process, and the locations where data is processed.
Customers may subscribe to updates about new or replacement sub-processors using the
sign-up form at the bottom of this page
