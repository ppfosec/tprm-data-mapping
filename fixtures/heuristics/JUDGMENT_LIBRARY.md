## TPRM-H-0001: Assess the engagement, not the vendor in isolation

- Status: proposed
- Version: 1.0.0
- GRC areas: third-party risk, business context, decision support
- Stages: S2 context, S4 synthesize
- Approved by: not yet approved

### Use when

A vendor finding is being treated as the decision.

### Understand

The vendor surface is shared. Exposure, value, ownership and the team's way of working decide what the same fact means here.

### Do

Attach the finding to a team, intended use and process before recommending action.

### Uncertainty

The engagement may be incomplete even when the vendor evidence is strong.

### Provenance

Normalized from the TPRM Lens product specification; awaiting separate public approval.

## TPRM-H-0002: Reconstruct sequence before calling it shadow IT

- Status: proposed
- Version: 1.0.0
- GRC areas: third-party risk, procurement, sequence analysis
- Stages: S1 sequence, S3 signals
- Approved by: not yet approved

### Use when

Purchase, access, data use and intake dates are available.

### Understand

Sequence distinguishes an early evaluation from a workflow already carrying customer data before review.

### Do

Order the events and name exactly what preceded review.

### Uncertainty

Missing identity or purchasing records can make a sequence look cleaner than it was.

### Provenance

Normalized from the TPRM Lens product specification; awaiting separate public approval.

## TPRM-H-0003: Contain the risky path without breaking the useful work

- Status: proposed
- Version: 1.0.0
- GRC areas: third-party risk, intervention design, adoption
- Stages: S5 intervene
- Approved by: not yet approved

### Use when

A useful workflow is already running outside the review path.

### Understand

A reflexive shutdown can move the behavior underground and destroy the relationship needed to correct it.

### Do

Assess the live use, contain the material data path, verify the vendor control and give the team a route to continue safely.

### Uncertainty

Immediate suspension remains appropriate when the material exposure cannot be bounded.

### Provenance

Normalized from Pierre-Paul's TPRM intervention direction; proposed for public review.

## TPRM-H-0004: Read the same vendor fact through team maturity

- Status: proposed
- Version: 1.0.0
- GRC areas: third-party risk, team maturity, intervention design
- Stages: S2 context, S5 intervene
- Approved by: not yet approved

### Use when

Two teams use the same vendor under different boundaries.

### Understand

A team that arrives with boundaries and trade-offs needs a decision. A team that arrives after launch needs containment and a relationship intervention.

### Do

Change the intervention, not the underlying vendor fact.

### Uncertainty

Fluency is not maturity; ground the appraisal in prior exchanges.

### Provenance

Adapted from the public Risk Lens maturity heuristic for TPRM; proposed.

## TPRM-H-0005: Refuse relationship judgment without history or intended use

- Status: proposed
- Version: 1.0.0
- GRC areas: third-party risk, analytical restraint, team maturity
- Stages: S2 context, S4 synthesize
- Approved by: not yet approved

### Use when

Fewer than two relevant interactions exist or intended use is unstated.

### Understand

Vendor documentation cannot fill in what the business plans to send or how the team will operate the relationship.

### Do

Abstain and ask only for the missing facts that change the decision. Do not call a model to invent maturity.

### Uncertainty

Relevant history may exist in a source that was not connected.

### Provenance

Adapted from the public Risk Lens abstention heuristic for TPRM; proposed.

## TPRM-H-0006: Treat vendor drift as a relationship event

- Status: proposed
- Version: 1.0.0
- GRC areas: third-party risk, continuous monitoring, contract management
- Stages: S3 signals, S5 intervene
- Approved by: not yet approved

### Use when

External terms or posture change after approval.

### Understand

The changed sentence matters through the data flow, renewal window, owner and authority that can accept it.

### Do

Reopen the affected engagement, name the changed trade-off and route it to the existing renewal work.

### Uncertainty

A public wording change may only clarify an unchanged operational practice.

### Provenance

Normalized from the Data Drift Detection and TPRM Lens specifications; proposed.
